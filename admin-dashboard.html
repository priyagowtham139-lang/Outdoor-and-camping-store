Use this when the item is inside the 60-night trial window and the fault is a manufacturing one. Confirm the order number, name the exact panel or part, give a dispatch date and close with the return label attached. Nothing here should ask the customer for a reason to return.

Acknowledge within four hours even if you cannot quote. Give the claim number, the bench date the photographs are due and a realistic turnaround of fourteen working days. Do not estimate a price before the technician has named the failure mode.

State the cause, not the process. If the parcel is more than one day past its promise date, offer the choice of a refund or a re-dispatch on the next trailer, and log the delay against the carrier record so the same root cause shows up in the weekly review.
