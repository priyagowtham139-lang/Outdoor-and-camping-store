Ask the trail team

Reno dispatches every order within 48 hours on weekdays. Standard is free above $120 in North America and Europe and $18 below that. Express is $24 and lands in two to three working days. Duties are calculated at checkout, so nothing is collected at your door.

  • Carbon-neutral road and sea freight on every line
  • Tracked and insured from shelf to summit

Sixty days, unused, in the original packaging, with no restocking fee. Send it back to Reno rather than the store and we email a prepaid label the moment the return is approved. Refunds land on the original payment method within five working days of the parcel being scanned.

On structural parts, yes: poles, frames, zips, buckles, hubs and seams. We cover manufacturing defects and normal wear for fifteen years and keep spare stock in the warehouse for exactly that reason. Cosmetic damage, third-party modifications and lost hardware are repaired at cost, never refused.

Tell us your height, the coldest temperature you expect and how you sleep, and we will cut a recommendation. The comfort rating we quote is the lowest temperature at which most people stay warm, not a survival figure. If you are between two sizes we will send the larger one and a liner, free.

Yes, including models retired more than a decade ago. Every part we have ever made is in the parts catalogue, owners get a permanent twenty per cent discount, and the door pocket of any Stackly shelter already contains the patch kit and pole sections needed for ninety per cent of field failures.

Digital cards from $25 to $500 arrive by email within fifteen minutes and never expire. They work online, in the Boulder fitting room and at any of our six stores. Partial balances are refunded to the original card if the card is lost, which we will confirm from the order email.

Open your account and the tracking link is on the order itself, updated at every carrier scan. If a parcel stalls for more than two working days, the support desk contacts the carrier for you rather than asking you to chase it. Parcels held at a depot beyond five days are re-dispatched at no cost.
