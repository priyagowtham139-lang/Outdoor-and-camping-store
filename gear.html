The Four Collections

Four series.
One load path.

A series is not a style. It is a tolerance envelope. Alpine runs 8.5 mm poles at 68 g/m for a 0.62 kg carried weight; Expedition runs 12.7 mm at 96 g/m and welds six reinforcement patches into every seam. Choose the series that matches your worst night, not your best one.

Series 01 / 3-season
Alpine Series 0.62–2.9 kg · 3 season · 9.4 m²
  • 8.5 mm 7001-T6 pole family

    Shock-corded 40 cm sections, ferrule-spliced in 90 seconds without a maul.

  • Gust envelope 90 km/h

    Tension tuned from a single continuous guyline loop, not six separate points.

  • Pole-sleeve topology

    Sleeves are external and half-open, so a crushed section is swapped without unpitching.

Materials & Guarantee

Three materials.
Fifteen years.

Ninety-one per cent of the range is built from three material families, chosen because they can all be repaired, re-sourced and eventually recycled in a domestic stream. No blended laminates, no PFC coatings, no proprietary zips.

Fabric Recycled 210D ripstop

Post-consumer nylon 6, 70 g/m², 6,000 mm hydrostatic head with a silicone-free PU face. Single polymer, so it goes back into the same stream it came from.
