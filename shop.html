Buying guide
Before you spend the money

Three questions decide it in about ninety seconds. If you still cannot choose, a gear tech will answer with a hard number.

Under 9 kg base weight, take the Trailpitch 1 at 1.42 kg. Between 9 and 14 kg, the Ridgeline 2 gives you the floor area you want without punishing the load. Above 14 kg you should be splitting party weight, not buying a bigger tent.

  • Under 9 kg: Trailpitch 1
  • 9 to 14 kg: Ridgeline 2
  • Snow loading: Vault 4 only

Below -5 °C with overnight snow on the roof, the two- and three-season shelters are the wrong purchase. Only the Vault 4 has a vaulted floor, snow skirts and poles rated to -30 °C. Anything warmer and you are paying 1.5 kg for a privilege you will not use.

Measure the floor at the shoulder, not the hip — the published dimensions are peak height and widest point. Two people in a three-person shelter is comfortable; three people in a four-season dome is roomy. Where you buy it does not change that.
