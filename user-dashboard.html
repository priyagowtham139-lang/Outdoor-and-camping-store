What saving an item does

Your list is held in this browser, not on a server, so nothing leaves this device and no account is required to browse.

Saved prices are compared against the live catalogue every hour. If an item drops, the heart icon switches to an outlined bell so you can decide without pressure.

The summary card beside your list carries a single button. Quantities are preserved if an item is already in the cart, and the list clears only after the transfer succeeds.

Because the list lives in local storage, wiping site data removes it permanently. Sign in and use the wishlist panel in the header if you want it to follow you between devices.
